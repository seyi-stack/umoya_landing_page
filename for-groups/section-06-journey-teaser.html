The Journey

Ten Days, Three Chapters

I

History & Culture

Johannesburg and Soweto, told by the people who lived it. Antoinette Sithole walks you through the day her brother died, and dinner is poured by a winemaker with her own label.

II

Safari

Three nights in the Sabi Sand at MalaMala, on land taken from its community under apartheid and won back through the courts. They own it today, and they welcome you onto it.

III

The Cape

Robben Island with Christo Brand, the warder who became Mandela's friend. Then the Winelands, the Atlantic Seaboard, and the food this city is known for.

Ten days, three provinces, welcomed at every turn by the people who call it home. Your group travels it together.
