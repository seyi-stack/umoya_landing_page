TRAVEL INSURANCE GUIDANCE

Why we require travel insurance, and what to look for in a policy


WHY WE REQUIRE TRAVEL INSURANCE

Comprehensive travel insurance is a requirement for every guest on every Umoya journey. The requirement exists for your protection, not ours. South Africa is far from most of our guests' home countries, the journey covers four provinces, and the value of a confirmed booking is meaningful. A good policy turns the small risks of international travel, a delayed flight, a forgotten medication, a missed connection, a family emergency at home, into administrative inconveniences rather than financial losses.

Our cancellation policy, set out in our Booking Terms and Conditions, is structured around the cancellation liabilities our suppliers hold us to. Insurance is what protects your investment in the gap between our policy and the circumstances that may arise outside it.


WHAT YOUR POLICY SHOULD COVER

We ask that every guest hold a policy covering, at a minimum, the following:

- Medical expenses overseas, including hospitalisation, evacuation, and repatriation. A minimum cover level of USD 100,000 is sensible for travel to South Africa.
- Trip cancellation and curtailment, covering circumstances that prevent you from travelling or require you to return home early. Confirm that the policy covers cancellation for the reasons most likely to affect you, including illness, injury, bereavement, and (where relevant) work or family obligations.
- Personal belongings and baggage, including delay, loss, and theft. Confirm the per-item limit is adequate for the equipment you intend to bring, particularly cameras, laptops, and jewellery.
- Travel delay and missed connection cover, which becomes relevant on long-haul itineraries with internal South African flights.
- Personal liability cover, in case of accidental injury to a third party or damage to property during your journey.


BUY YOUR POLICY AT THE TIME OF BOOKING

The single most common mistake travelers make is buying insurance close to the departure date. Cancellation cover only takes effect once the policy is purchased. If you book a journey in March for travel in November, and a circumstance arises in June that requires you to cancel, a policy purchased in October will not help you. We strongly recommend that you take out your policy within a few days of confirming your booking.

Some policies also offer "cancel for any reason" cover at additional cost, which extends protection to reasons not listed in the standard policy. This is worth considering for premium journeys where the financial exposure is meaningful and your reasons for needing to cancel may not fit a tidy category.


WHAT OUR CANCELLATION POLICY ALREADY DOES, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT DO

Our Booking Terms and Conditions set out the cancellation schedule that applies when you cancel a confirmed booking. The schedule is structured around when you cancel relative to your departure date, and reflects the deposits and balances we have committed to suppliers on your behalf.

What our policy does not do is protect you against the cost of cancelling because of circumstances outside your control: illness, family emergencies, work events, government travel advisories, missed connecting flights from home. That is the work of travel insurance.


CHOOSING A PROVIDER

Insurance is regulated in each country, and the best provider for you will depend on where you live. A few general principles:

- Choose a policy written for international travel from your country of residence, not one offered in-flight or at the airport.
- Read the exclusions carefully. Pre-existing medical conditions, certain activities (including some safari activities), and travel against your government's advisories may be excluded by default.
- Confirm that South Africa is included in the geographic scope of your policy. A small number of policies treat parts of Africa as restricted regions.
- Keep a copy of your policy and the 24-hour emergency assistance number with you during your journey, and share them with your travelling companion or a family member at home.


WHAT WE WILL ASK YOU FOR BEFORE DEPARTURE

Before your departure date, we will ask you to confirm:

- The name of your insurance provider.
- Your policy number.
- The 24-hour emergency assistance number issued by your insurer.

We do not ask for the full policy document. We hold these details solely so that we, or our in-country team, can support you if you need to make a claim while you are travelling with us.


IF YOU HAVE QUESTIONS

If you are unsure whether a particular policy meets our minimum requirements, or you have a question about a specific situation, please reach out. We are not insurance brokers and cannot recommend a specific provider, but we are happy to look at a policy summary and let you know whether what we can see covers the journey you have booked.
