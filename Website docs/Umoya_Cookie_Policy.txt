1. ABOUT THIS POLICY

This Cookie Policy explains how Umoya Afrika Tours (Pty) Ltd ("Umoya", "we", "us") uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy, which sets out how we process personal information more generally.


2. WHAT ARE COOKIES?

A cookie is a small text file that is stored on your device when you visit a website. Cookies allow a website to recognise your device, remember your preferences and understand how you use the site. Similar technologies, such as web beacons and pixels, perform comparable functions and are treated as cookies for the purposes of this policy.


3. THE COOKIES WE USE

We use the following categories of cookies on our website.

Strictly necessary
Purpose: Enable core website functionality, including security, page navigation and the use of forms. The website cannot function properly without these cookies.
Consent required: No

Performance and analytics
Purpose: Help us understand how visitors use our website, including which pages are visited and how visitors move through the site, so that we can improve the experience.
Consent required: Yes

Functional
Purpose: Remember preferences you have selected so that we can offer you a more personalised experience on return visits.
Consent required: Yes

Marketing
Purpose: Used by us or our advertising partners to measure the effectiveness of our marketing and, where applicable, to show you content that may be of interest.
Consent required: Yes

Where we use HubSpot or other marketing and analytics tools, those tools may set performance, functional or marketing cookies, which require your consent.


4. YOUR CHOICES

When you first visit our website, you are presented with a cookie banner that allows you to accept or reject non-essential cookies. You can change your preferences at any time by clicking the cookie-settings link on our website.

Most browsers also allow you to manage cookies through your browser settings. You can delete cookies that are already on your device and prevent new cookies from being set. Please be aware that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of the website.
