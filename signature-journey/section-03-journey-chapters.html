Chapter One

History & Culture

Johannesburg & Soweto

Where the country's recent history is closest to the surface, and its creative present most alive. You begin with a warm welcome, then spend a full day in Soweto with the township's own people, before standing where the story of freedom was written.

The Welcome

Lucia Motloung

A welcome dinner hosted by the winemaker behind Luc Mo, built with the Ndebele Kingdom.

The Soweto Day

Antoinette Sithole

A full day through Soweto by tuk-tuk, with local guides, and lunch hosted by the sister of Hector Pieterson.

The Story

Mandela House & the Apartheid Museum

The places where the struggle was lived, told plainly and with care.

Chapter Two

Safari

The Sabi Sand

Into the iconic safari country of the lowveld. Three nights at MalaMala, a private Big Five reserve returned to the community that lives there, with open-vehicle game drives morning and evening, and an afternoon given to the people whose home this land is.

The Wild

Big Five Game Drives

Morning and evening in open vehicles with private field guides, unhurried and close.

The Land

A Legacy Restored

MalaMala was taken from its community during apartheid. Through decades of determination, they fought for it, won it back, and today they welcome you onto it.

The People

An Afternoon Beyond the Reserve

Time with the community that lives alongside the wilderness.

Chapter Three

The Cape

Cape Town

The journey closes where the land meets two oceans. Robben Island and Table Mountain, the Cape Peninsula, a day among the Black-owned wineries of the Cape, and a farewell worthy of the journey behind it.

Robben Island

Christo Brand

The warder who guarded Nelson Mandela, and became his friend, walks you through himself.

The Black Route

Vivian Kleynhans

A day among Black-owned wineries, with the CEO of Seven Sisters Vineyards, a pioneer who established the prominent, 100% black-owned wine brand in Stellenbosch.

The Farewell

A Closing Dinner

A final evening with live performance, to mark the journey behind you.
