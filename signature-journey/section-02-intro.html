The Journey

See South Africa the Way it Deserves to be Seen

The Signature Journey moves as one arc across South Africa. It begins with the country's living history in Johannesburg and Soweto, opens into the Big Five wilderness of the Sabi Sand, and closes in the beauty of the Cape. An optional extension carries you on to Victoria Falls and Chobe.
