The Journey

See South Africa the way it deserves to be seen.

The Signature Journey moves as one arc across South Africa. It begins with the country's living history in Johannesburg and Soweto, opens into the Big Five wilderness of the Sabi Sand, and closes in the beauty of the Cape. An optional extension carries you on to Victoria Falls and Chobe.

Chapter One

History & Culture

Johannesburg & Soweto

Where the country's recent history is closest to the surface, and its creative present most alive. You begin with a warm welcome, then spend a full day in Soweto with the township's own people, before standing where the story of freedom was written.

Two Optional Extensions

Experiences Beyond South Africa

For those who wish to travel on. Both extensions are arranged seamlessly as part of your journey, and Extension Two builds on Extension One.

Extension One · A World Wonder

Victoria Falls

Victoria Falls, Zimbabwe

The journey carries north to Victoria Falls, one of the seven natural wonders of the world, on the border of Zimbabwe and Zambia. Feel the mist, hear the thunder, and stand close enough to the falls to understand why they are called the smoke that thunders. You stay 3 nights on the banks of the Zambezi at Victoria Falls River Lodge, with a sunset cruise on the river, a guided tour of the falls, and a Boma dinner under the stars.

Extension Two · Africa's Eden

Victoria Falls & Chobe

Chobe, Botswana

Everything in Extension One, then you cross into Botswana and finish on the water, aboard a private houseboat on the Chobe River. Drift at golden hour as elephants gather on the banks in numbers found almost nowhere else on earth, and close the journey on the river itself.
