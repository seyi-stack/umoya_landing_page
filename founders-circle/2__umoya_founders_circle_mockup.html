Our Approach

A new company, built on long relationships.

It is built on people we know by name.

Umoya is African-led and African-owned, and of the diaspora ourselves. Wilson Nyah built it over a decade of work across the continent, and on the relationships that came with it. Those relationships are why Lucia Motloung pours her own wine on your first evening, and why Antoinette Sithole sits at your table in Soweto and tells you about the day in 1976 she lived herself.

Around those moments, the work is ours. You are met at the airport, a host stays with you the whole way, and what would normally be yours to organise is carried for you. You arrive as a guest of people who are glad to share their home.

Everything You Need to Know

Travel essentials.

Plan with absolute confidenceGuaranteed departures
+
Once your departure is confirmed, it is guaranteed. From that point you can book flights and make arrangements knowing your journey will proceed as planned.
Your journey, your wayThoughtful personalization
+
Each itinerary is carefully designed, and yours can flex to your interests. Optional experiences let you add private tastings, extended time at a site, or access to a particular host.
Peace of mind for your investmentTravel protection
+
We strongly recommend travel insurance at the time of booking. Your travel expert can guide you to reputable providers so you can focus on the journey, not the logistics.
Paced for depthSignature Journey structure
+
The Signature Journey runs ten days across three movements, Heritage, Safari, and the Cape, with an optional extension to Victoria Falls and Chobe. This pacing is what gives the journey its depth.
Flavours that meet your needsRegional cuisine & dietary care
+
You taste regional cuisine prepared by local hosts and chefs throughout. Dietary needs are collected in advance and handled with care, because your wellbeing is part of the journey.
