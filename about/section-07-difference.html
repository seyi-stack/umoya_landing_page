The Umoya Difference

What makes an Umoya journey distinct?

Welcomed by name

Our journeys are built on relationships earned in person, on the ground. The people who host you are artists, entrepreneurs, activists, and winemakers who open their world to you directly.

Bespoke from the start

Every Umoya journey is designed around you. Your group, your interests, your pace. We shape the experience around what matters to you and handle every detail along the way.

Recognized for storytelling

Our destination film, Let Your Story Begin, earned a Silver Award at the International Tourism Film Festival Africa 2026. Narrative-led travel shapes every journey we design.

Leave the logistics to us

Planning a journey should be the easy part. We handle every detail from start to finish, so that by the time you arrive, all you have to do is breathe.
