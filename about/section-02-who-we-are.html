Who We Are

Umoya means breath, or wind.

Umoya (oo-MOH-ya), isiZulu

It is the feeling an Umoya journey is built to give you from the moment you arrive: a country that opens up and lets you breathe it in.

We design premium, tailor-made journeys across South Africa, with cultural depth, named hosts, and seamless execution. Every journey brings together the country's history, food, wine, music, and creative scene, delivered with the finest lodges and looked after in every detail.
