The Umoya Signature Journey moves through three immersive chapters, from the living history of Johannesburg and Soweto, where you meet the people who lived the story, to the wild beauty of the Sabi Sand, where Africa's great animals roam freely across land returned to its rightful community. It ends at the dramatic meeting of mountain and ocean in Cape Town, with optional extensions to the thundering spectacle of Victoria Falls and the elephant herds of Chobe. A journey shaped with intention, moving through heritage, wilderness, and wonder, and offering a depth of access most travelers never find.

  • Itinerary at a Glance: Johannesburg & Soweto, the Sabi Sand, and Cape Town — with optional extensions to Victoria Falls and Chobe.
