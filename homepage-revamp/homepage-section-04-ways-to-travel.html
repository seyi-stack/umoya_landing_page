Ways to Travel

Travel Designed
With You in Mind

Large Groups

Corporate, organizational, or group travel designed around you. From incentive trips and leadership retreats to alumni gatherings and community travel, we handle every detail so your group simply arrives and experiences.

Solo Travel

Arrive alone, leave with connections that last. Umoya's small groups bring together like-minded travelers, so the people you meet along the way become as much a part of the journey as the places you see.

Couples Getaway

Some places are simply better experienced together. From sundowners in the bush to private wine tastings in the Cape, every moment of the Umoya journey is designed to be shared, in settings that stay with you long after you return home.

World Wonders

For those who want to go further and stay longer, extend your journey into Africa's most breathtaking destinations. Victoria Falls and the vast elephant herds of Chobe are waiting, and we take care of everything to get you there.

Safari

The African bush is unlike anywhere else on earth, and the Sabi Sand is one of the finest places to experience it. With expert guides, open vehicles, and the freedom to follow the animals wherever they go, the Big 5 come to you on your own terms.

Winelands

The Cape Winelands move at their own unhurried pace, and that is exactly how we explore them. Private tastings, long lunches, and conversations with the Black winemakers rewriting the story of South African wine, straight from the source.
