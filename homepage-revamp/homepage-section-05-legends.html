The Umoya Difference

Signature Moments Guided by South African Legends

At Umoya Afrika Tours, our journeys are shaped by local legends, not just guides. Throughout each chapter, you will be introduced to South African artists, entrepreneurs, and activists who are still shaping the country, and who share their stories with you directly. These are not arranged encounters. They are rare, personal moments with people whose lives sit at the heart of what makes this country extraordinary, and they are what make an Umoya journey unlike anything else.

The Welcome

Lucia Motloung

She welcomes you on your first evening, pouring wines from Luc Mo, the company she built in partnership with the Ndebele Kingdom.

Soweto

Antoinette Sithole

Her brother was Hector Pieterson, the boy in the 1976 photograph of the anti-apartheid riots. She sits at your table and tells you about the day she lost her brother.

Robben Island

Christo Brand

He spent years as Nelson Mandela's warder, and the two became friends. He walks you through the island and tells you what that looked like up close.

The Black Winelands

Ntsiki Biyela

South Africa's first Black female winemaker. She pours her own wines for you to experience and takes you through a sommelier experience.
