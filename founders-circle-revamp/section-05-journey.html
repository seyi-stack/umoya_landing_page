Three immersive chapters, from the culture and history of Johannesburg, to safari in the Sabi Sand, to the Cape.

History & Culture

Johannesburg & Soweto

A warm welcome from Lucia Motloung, a CEO of black-owned wine company Luc Mo Wines, ride through the colorful streets of Soweto, where history and culture come to light before your eyes. Have lunch with Antoinette Sithole, sister of the late Hector Pieterson whose legacy in the anti-apartheid movement lives on.

Safari Game Drives

The Sabi Sand

There are few places on earth where you can watch lions, leopards, elephants, buffalo, and rhinos move freely through the wild. Days in the Sabi Sand follow the rhythm of the bush: early morning game drives as the light breaks, and golden-hour sundowners as the day slows down.

The Cape

Cape Town

Experience the city where mountain meets ocean: Robben Island with Christo Brand, the warder who once guarded Mandela, and a day on the Black-owned Winelands route with Ntsiki Biyela, the first Black woman winemaker in South Africa.

Optional Extensions

Experiences Beyond South Africa

A World Wonder

Victoria Falls

The journey extends north to Victoria Falls, one of the world's seven natural wonders straddling the border of Zambia and Zimbabwe. Feel the mist, hear the thunder, and experience the majesty of these incredible falls up-close. Enjoy dinner over the Zambezi river and be in awe as you dine over the fourth-largest river in Africa.

Discover Africa's Eden

Chobe National Park, Botswana

Cross into Botswana and experience wilderness like never before. Drift down the Chobe River at golden hour on a boat cruise as elephants gather on the banks in numbers found almost nowhere else on earth. A rare ending that outdoes everything before it.
