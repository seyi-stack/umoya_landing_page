1. ABOUT THIS POLICY

This Cookie Policy explains how Umoya Afrika Tours (Pty) Ltd ("Umoya", "we", "us") uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy, which sets out how we process personal information more generally.


2. WHAT ARE COOKIES?

A cookie is a small text file that is stored on your device when you visit a website. Cookies allow a website to recognise your device, remember your preferences and understand how you use the site. Similar technologies, such as web beacons and pixels, perform comparable functions and are treated as cookies for the purposes of this policy.

Like most websites, ours uses both first-party cookies (set by us) and third-party cookies (set by services we use to run the site, understand how it performs, and keep it secure).


3. THE COOKIES WE USE

We use the following categories of cookies on our website. The specific cookies currently set on your visit, together with their name, provider, purpose and duration, are listed in our cookie preferences panel and are updated automatically by our consent management platform, CookieYes.

Necessary

Necessary cookies are required to enable the basic features of this site, including security, page navigation, form use, and the storage of your consent preferences. The website cannot function properly without these cookies, and they do not require your consent.

The Necessary cookies currently set on our website include:

- __cf_bm (Cloudflare, 1 hour) - supports Cloudflare's bot management service, which protects the site from automated abuse.
- m (Stripe, approximately 1 year) - set by Stripe to identify the device you are using, for fraud-prevention purposes, whether or not you complete a payment.
- A cookie set by the website's page-building engine (session-based) - allows us to publish and update site content.
- A cookie related to display formatting (session-based) - ensures the site renders correctly across browsers.
- The CookieYes consent cookie - stores the cookie preferences you set on your first visit, so that we do not ask you again.

Performance and analytics

These cookies help us understand how visitors use our website, including which pages are visited, how visitors arrive at the site, and how they move through it, so that we can improve the experience. They require your consent.

The Performance and analytics cookies currently set on our website include:

- _ga_* (Google Analytics, approximately 1 year) - used by Google Analytics to count and record page views, enabling us to understand overall site traffic and visitor behaviour in aggregate.

We may also use HubSpot analytics tools in support of our marketing and communications work, which set additional cookies in this category from time to time.

Functional

These cookies remember preferences you have selected, such as language or region, so that we can offer you a more personalised experience on return visits. They require your consent.

Marketing

These cookies are used by us or our advertising partners to measure the effectiveness of our marketing and, where applicable, to show you content that may be of interest. They require your consent.

We do not currently run advertising campaigns that set marketing cookies. This category will be updated if that changes.


4. YOUR CHOICES

When you first visit our website, you are presented with a cookie banner that allows you to accept all cookies, reject non-essential cookies, or manage your preferences by category. You can change your preferences at any time by clicking the cookie-settings link in the footer of our website.

Our website does not currently respond to Do Not Track browser signals. You can manage your cookie preferences using the tools described above, or through your browser settings, which allow you to delete cookies that are already on your device and to prevent new cookies from being set. Please be aware that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of the website.

Your browser's own cookie controls are documented here:

- Chrome: https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/32050
- Safari: https://support.apple.com/en-in/guide/safari/sfri11471/mac
- Firefox: https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/clear-cookies-and-site-data-firefox
- Edge: https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/delete-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-63947406-40ac-c3b8-57b9-2a946a29ae09

If you use a different browser, please refer to its official support documentation.
